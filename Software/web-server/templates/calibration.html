{# ---------------- Lens ---------------- #}

Lens

Measures how the lens bends the picture so PiTrac can straighten it. Redo it only after changing or refocusing a lens.

  1. Print the pattern at 100% scale, not "fit to page".
  2. Measure one square with a ruler. It should be 23 mm across. If not, fix the printer scale and print again.
  3. Mount the sheet flat on stiff card or foam board. Any bend spoils the result.
  4. Camera 2 only: take off its IR-pass filter so it can see the pattern. Put it back when you are done.
  5. Stop PiTrac from the header.

{# ---------------- Position ---------------- #}

Position

Measures where each camera points so PiTrac can place the ball in 3D. Redo it after moving or bumping a camera.

  1. Set up the calibration rig in front of PiTrac.
  2. Camera 1: put one ball on the floor at the Ball 1 spot.
  3. Camera 2: put one ball at the Ball 2 spot on the rig, in mid-air.
  4. Stop PiTrac from the header.
